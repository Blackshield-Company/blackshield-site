Known issues & fixes

For the Blackshield Company Skyrim SE mods. Back to all mods

Mods by synthalorian (Blackshield Company) for Skyrim Special Edition.

Last updated: 17 Oct 2026

All of our mods are early beta. They pass our automated build checks but haven't been fully playtested in game, so player reports matter a lot. Updates are save-safe unless a fix says otherwise. Make a fresh save before you update, just in case.

Translators: always build from the latest version. A translated plugin saved from an older version overrides the newer fixes and brings old bugs back. Keep text tags such as <Global=TTC_BribeGold> or <Alias=...> exactly as written, and translate the words around them.

The Turncoat Contract

Current version: 0.1.4 (16 Oct 2026)

Corporal Vane's bribe line shows <Global=TTC_BribeGold> instead of a gold amount

  • Symptom: When you talk to Corporal Vane, her line reads something like "<Global=TTC_BribeGold> gold and my ring..." instead of a number.
  • Affected: 0.1.0–0.1.3
  • Fixed in: 0.1.4
  • Workaround: Update to 0.1.4. Skyrim doesn't fill in this kind of tag in NPC subtitles, so the amount now appears in your reply: "Make me an offer. (1500 gold)". It still follows the TTC_BribeGold setting. Older saves keep working. Translators: rebuild from 0.1.4.
  • Reported by a player.

Dialogue or the contract writ shows a raw location tag (like <Alias=AmbushLoc>) instead of the place name

  • Symptom: When you talk to the quest giver again, or pick a dialogue option after accepting, you see a raw tag instead of a place name. The map marker still works.
  • Affected: versions before 0.1.3 (the accept line before 0.1.2; later dialogue options and notes until 0.1.3)
  • Fixed in: 0.1.2 (accept line) and 0.1.3 (later dialogue and notes)
  • Workaround: Update to 0.1.4. If you still see a raw tag, post a screenshot on the Bugs tab with your mod version and whether you use a translation.
  • Reported by a player.

Quest stuck because the captain didn't have the payroll ledger

  • Symptom: The ledger you need isn't on the captain, so the quest can't move on.
  • Affected: versions before 0.1.1
  • Fixed in: 0.1.1 (the ledger is now given by script and rechecked)
  • Workaround: With the sealed orders in your inventory, tell Hjalla "the captain didn't have your ledger". She'll sort you out and the quest continues (0.1.1 and later, works on old saves).

Company Quartermaster

Current version: 0.2.4 (15 Oct 2026)

OPEN: Brynja's face or skin colour not matching

  • Symptom: Brynja's face and body don't match. On 0.2.1 her arms, midriff and legs rendered black or dark brown while her face looked normal. On 0.2.2 her body was fixed but her face rendered dark grey.
  • Affected: 0.2.1, 0.2.2
  • Fixed in: 0.2.4 (fix shipped). Status: Open, because we're waiting for the reporter to confirm it's gone.
  • What 0.2.4 changed: 0.2.2 gave her body a skin tone that matches her face. 0.2.4 gives her a vanilla Nord look using an Uthgerd template: she takes Uthgerd's face, hair, skin tone and voice. Her name, outfit, behaviour and scripts are unchanged, and no form IDs changed, so saves keep working. The fix applies the next time she loads.
  • Workaround: 1. Update to 0.2.4. 2. If you use a translated plugin, it must be rebuilt from 0.2.4. An old translated plugin overrides Brynja and brings back her old look. 3. Check whether anything else in your load order, such as a FaceGen or NPC appearance pack, changes Brynja (CQM_Brynja, local 000820). 4. Still wrong after all that? Post on the Bugs tab with a screenshot and your load order.
  • Reported by a player.

Brynja looks like a different bandit every time she's hired

  • Symptom: Each time you summon or hire her she has a different face and gear.
  • Affected: versions before 0.2.1 (reported on 0.2.0)
  • Fixed in: 0.2.1 (she's now a unique NPC with one fixed look, her own iron armour and a sword)
  • Workaround: Update. If you hired her on an older save, dismiss her and hire her again to pick up the fixed look.
  • Reported by a player.

Roadwarden

Current version: 0.1.1 (10 Oct 2026)

I never see any encounters

  • Symptom: You travel the roads but no encounters ever happen.
  • Affected: 0.1.0 (too strict: riding a horse blocked encounters, big stretches of road were filtered out, and encounters were rare)
  • Fixed in: 0.1.1 (mounted travel counts, fewer roads are excluded, encounters are more frequent, and a "Roadwarden active" notice appears on load)
  • Workaround: Update to 0.1.1 and look for "Roadwarden active" when you load. Fast travel and carriages never trigger encounters. Still nothing? In the console (~): 1. getqr RWD_RoadQuest should say 1. If it says 0, the mod isn't running, so please report it. 2. On an older save, type show RWD_ChancePct. If it still shows the old values, set RWD_CheckSeconds to 45, RWD_ChancePct to 15 and RWD_CooldownHours to 4. 3. On foot, outside town, walk a road for about 30 seconds, then set RWD_ForceNext to 1 and keep walking. Highwaymen should appear in about 10 seconds. 4. If that fails, turn on Papyrus logging and report the RWD_ lines with your game version.

How to report a bug

Use the Bugs tab on the mod's Nexus page (author: synthalorian) and include:

  1. The mod and its version (for example, Contract Hunter 0.1.3) and your game version (1.5.97 or 1.6.x)
  2. Your load order (a LOOT-sorted list or a screenshot from your mod manager is fine)
  3. Steps to reproduce: what you did, what you expected, and what happened. For text or appearance problems, add a screenshot.
  4. If you can, the Papyrus log lines that start with the mod's prefix (for example TTC_, CQM_ or RWD_). Turn on logging in Skyrim.ini under [Papyrus] with bEnableLogging=1 and bEnableTrace=1.

We read every report. Thanks for helping make these mods better!
