The workshop

One crest. One developer.

Blackshield Company is the workshop of synthalorian. Linux tools, games, audio, and Skyrim mods share one bench. A tool waits until there is a way back. A mod ships light. The knight is the mark on the work, not a staff list.

Nothing in the shop is for sale yet. Mods on Nexus are free, and each one still in early beta is marked beta.
